Professional Profile

Royal College-certified pediatrician and pediatric gastroenterologist with Canadian residency and fellowship training and a broad clinical background in pediatric gastroenterology, hepatology, nutrition, endoscopy, motility and functional gastrointestinal disorders. Clinical interests include inflammatory bowel disease, eosinophilic gastrointestinal disorders, complex constipation and fecal incontinence, feeding and nutrition disorders, gastrointestinal manifestations of neurodevelopmental conditions, pediatric liver disease, gastrointestinal bleeding, and virtual/in-person consultative care.

Experienced in diagnostic and interventional pediatric endoscopy, enteral-access procedures, capsule endoscopy, pH-impedance testing, hydrogen/methane breath testing, anorectal manometry, and percutaneous liver biopsy. Practice experience spans Canada, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, and earlier military/aerospace medicine settings, with ongoing involvement in teaching, quality improvement, resident education, case-based learning, and clinical research.

His practice is grounded in comprehensive, family-centered pediatric gastroenterology and a broad pediatric foundation, including general pediatrics, emergency pediatrics, inpatient pediatrics, newborn assessment, Level II nursery exposure, nutrition, growth, virtual care workflows, and multidisciplinary coordination.
